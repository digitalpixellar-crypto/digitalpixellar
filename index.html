COMMERCIAL ENGAGEMENT FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers about our engagement models, delivery timelines, and commercial accountability.

What makes Digital Pixellar different from traditional marketing agencies?

Most agencies are fragmented: they offer isolated ad boosting without tech infrastructure, or web development without marketing attribution. Digital Pixellar unites commercial strategy, bespoke design, custom software, and AI automation under one roof under Yeswanth Technologies. We operate as an aligned commercial growth partner with transparent unit economics.

How does your real estate marketing engine work?

We build full-funnel customer acquisition ecosystems tailored specifically for luxury villas, gated communities, plotting layouts, and commercial hubs. This combines intent-driven Meta and Google search ads with instant automated WhatsApp brochure delivery (<6 seconds response) and automated CRM pipeline routing to your sales team.

Who owns the ad accounts, digital assets, and source code?

You do. 100%. We operate transparently within your own business manager, ad accounts, and repositories. You maintain permanent ownership of all creative assets, tracking pixels, customer data, and source code at all times.

How quickly can an engagement go live?

Strategic discovery and technical audits typically complete within 5 to 7 business days. Campaign creative production, tracking attribution setup, and automated CRM pipelines launch within 10 to 14 business days from kickoff.
